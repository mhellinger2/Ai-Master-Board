# SAM.gov Registration Packet
Prepared 2026-11-15 for the First Federal Contract goal. Nothing has been registered or submitted. Registration is Michael's own action (it needs his EIN and banking details, which he enters himself).

## Decision needed first: which entity registers

SAM registration is tied to one legal entity and its EIN. Switching entities later means a brand new registration and a new 10 to 15 business day validation, so this choice is worth making once.

**Option A: Hellinger Homes LLC (existing)**
- Fastest path. EIN and a business bank account already exist, so registration can happen in one sitting.
- Downsides: the SAM entity record is publicly searchable, so the photography LLC name and address become attached to federal bidding. Reselling income and liability mix into the same LLC as the photography business. A DBA can soften the name issue but does not separate liability.

**Option B: New LLC for reselling**
- Cleanest separation: its own EIN, bank account, books, and liability wall between reselling and photography.
- Downsides: NC Articles of Organization filing fee ($125), then EIN, then a bank account, so days to weeks before SAM registration can even start. One more entity to maintain (annual report, separate taxes).

**Option C: Redline Measurements LLC (existing)**
- EIN process is already the next step for Red Line, and measuring is a services brand, not products. Mixing a product reselling operation into it muddies both. Not recommended.

**Recommendation:** If the goal is testing viability with the least friction, Option A starts the SAM clock now and costs nothing; entity separation can be revisited after the first contract proves the model. If protecting the photography business matters more than speed, Option B is the right structure and the delay is the price of it. Either way, decide before registering, because the EIN used at registration is permanent for that registration.

## One-sitting registration checklist

Have all of this in front of you before starting at sam.gov. Registration is free; never pay a third party for it.

1. Login.gov account (create one or use an existing one; this becomes the SAM administrator login).
2. Legal business name and physical address exactly as they appear on IRS records. A mismatch is the most common cause of validation delays.
3. EIN / TIN for the chosen entity, plus the IRS confirmation letter if available (name and address on the letter are what SAM validates against).
4. Business bank account number and routing number for EFT payments (the government pays by direct deposit only).
5. Business start date and fiscal year end (from the LLC's formation records).
6. NAICS codes (suggested set below; you can list several, one is marked primary).
7. A point of contact name, email, and phone for the entity administrator and government business POC (can be the same person: you).
8. Entity type and size information: LLC, number of employees (1), annual receipts (your business revenue range).

After submitting: IRS TIN validation, then CAGE code assignment by DoD. Average activation is 10 to 15 business days. Renew every 365 days.

## Suggested NAICS codes for product reselling

- 423210 Furniture Merchant Wholesalers (primary candidate if furniture stays the target category)
- 424120 Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers
- 423420 Office Equipment Merchant Wholesalers
- 423990 Other Miscellaneous Durable Goods Merchant Wholesalers
- 423450 Medical, Dental, and Hospital Equipment Merchant Wholesalers (only if that category is ever targeted)

Final category choice follows the first product category decision; codes can be added at renewal or by updating the registration.

## After SAM activates

1. PIEE (Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment) vendor account. Several solicitations accept quotes only through PIEE; it needs its own registration linked to the SAM entity.
2. Pick one simple product category and line up a distributor account (winners bid from distributor pricing, not retail).
3. Bid only pure product RFQs first (delivery only, no installation or services), and expect a low win rate while learning.
