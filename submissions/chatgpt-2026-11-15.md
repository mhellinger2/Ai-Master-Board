Bottom line: make the entity decision a deliberate business-structure decision rather than defaulting to the fastest registration path.

Legitimacy: ethanplusai/sellbird (formerly Harvey, renamed v0.4.0 Nov 6, 2026), AGPL-3.0, roughly 5,300 stars and 1,100 forks, 538 commits, dedicated security policy, documented architecture and tests. Buying-signal scan: deterministic website profiling, not model-scored. Pipeline: Discover (businesses matching an ideal customer profile, via OpenStreetMap and optional paid providers), Profile (HTTP requests to the company website), Evaluate signals (missing online booking, active advertising, relevant job openings), Enrich (owners/managers from published info), Verify (email patterns and deliverability), Generate outreach. Website scanning needs no model calls and no model costs. Signals are indicators, not proof of purchasing intent.

Fit with Michael's rules: discovery, signal scanning, owner identification, and email verification are compatible. Email drafts optional. Automatic sending is prohibited under his rules (Sellbird has an autopilot mode that must remain disabled). DM-first outreach is not supported natively. Do not connect Michael's primary inbox.

Worth adopting (for testing): (A) buying-signal intelligence to prioritize the existing DM-first outreach, (B) decision-maker research, (C) evidence-based personalization for opening messages.

Conditions before adopting anything: clone and inspect rather than blindly running install instructions; isolated environment with no primary inbox access; no Gmail/SMTP/sending credentials entered; sending and reply handling disabled; discovery, profiling, and CSV export only; review AGPL-3.0 before reusing code; validate signal classifications against manually inspected websites. Noted positive: Sellbird documents SSRF protections on its profiler (redirects to local/private addresses), a good engineering sign, not an audit.

Final recommendation: conditional adoption as a research tool only. Sellbird finds reasons to contact businesses; Michael's existing system decides how they are approached.
